Why antcrew

Built for teams that need to trust what their agents do

Not a chatbot wrapper. A typed, traceable pipeline of specialised agents that integrates into your workflow without losing visibility or control.

Typed agent contracts
Agents communicate through Pydantic-validated schemas. A malformed PRD is rejected at the gate — not at runtime, three agents later.
TraceLog & Replay
Every run writes a structured audit log. Replay any run exactly — same state, same inputs, same agent decisions — to debug a failure or demonstrate compliance.
Your infra, your keys
BYOK with OpenAI, Anthropic, Azure, or Ollama. Or run fully self-hosted. Code and context never leave your environment — required for fintech, health, and European teams.
Human in the loop
Define approval checkpoints at any stage. Agents pause and route for human review before committing changes. You keep the final word on what ships.
Compliance Pack

Ready for regulated industries

Healthcare, finance, and legal teams need more than logs. antcrew ships with run attestation, governance hashes, HMAC signing, and BYOK data residency out of the box.

Read the compliance guide →
  • Run attestation
    Cryptographically signed provenance for every run. SHA-256 + HMAC. Verifiable without platform access.
  • Governance hashes
    Detect prompt drift across runs. Prove that the production agent is the reviewed, approved version.
  • BYOK & data residency
    Your keys, your region, your infra. Data never leaves your environment. Required for GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2.
